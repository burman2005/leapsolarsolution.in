FAQ

Questions, answered

How much does a rooftop solar system cost?

A typical 3 kW home system costs roughly ₹1.8–2 lakh before subsidy. With the central subsidy, your net cost can drop significantly. We share an exact quote after the site survey.

Am I eligible for a government subsidy?

Most residential grid-connected systems qualify under PM Surya Ghar. We check eligibility and file the application for you.

How long does installation take?

Residential installs take 2–5 days. Net metering approval by the DISCOM usually takes 2–4 weeks.

Will solar work during cloudy days or power cuts?

Panels still generate on cloudy days at reduced output. On-grid systems shut off during outages for safety; hybrid systems with batteries keep running.

What maintenance is needed?

Regular cleaning (every 2–4 weeks in NCR) and an annual check-up. We offer AMC plans covering both.
